# Phone widget beats app
**Claim:** *For a chore reminder, a home-screen widget is more useful than the app itself.*
## Because
I never open [[Plant Parent]], but I see my home screen every day.
## Impact
- [[Watering streak widget]] becomes the main feature, not an extra.
- The app only needs settings and an add-plant screen.
